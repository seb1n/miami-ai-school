04 / FORMAL EVALUATION

Test it after review

A machine check is a signal, not proof of truth. The rubric still needs a human. A script without a current MP4 cannot pass the full workflow checks.

First record a decision for each draft, then run the checks.

Human rubric

Hard gates first: verify every claim and quote against its source, preserve meaning and constraints, inspect the actual current MP4, and require version-matched approval. Any hard-gate failure requires revision.

  1. Clarity: can a beginner understand it?
  2. Audience fit: is it right for busy nontechnical professionals?
  3. Usefulness: is the main idea and next step worth their attention?

Score each soft dimension 0 (misses), 1 (partial), or 2 (strong). Target at least 5/6, with every hard gate passing. Record your scores in the workbook or review notes. A high soft score never overrides an unsupported fact, missing media or stale approval.
